I assume here that I do not need to spend a lot of time 
introducing concepts like Data Transparency and 
open-access data sets.  Long story short, 
increasingly the basic unit of dissemination for 
academic scholarship and scientific research is not 
the individual text publication, but a package that 
can contain data, text, computer code, and 
multimedia presentations.
