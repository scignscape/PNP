\begin{lightquadblockc}{1,0.4,0.1}{\parbox{21cm}{\centering \vspace{13pt}\color[rgb]{0,0,0}{According 
to the Diamond (DOA) model, publications have neither 
Article Processing Charges (APCs) nor paywalls}\vspace{8pt}}}
\hspace{-4pt}\begin{minipage}{1.07\textwidth}
{\fontsize{16}{22}\selectfont \setlength{\leftmargini}{30pt}\begin{enumerate}

\dmitem \textbf{Authors Retain Copyright} \hspace{.5em} No fees for either authors or readers. 
Authors can share their work in any form (PDF, XML, HTML, etc.) \vspace{12pt} 

\dmitem \textbf{Unify Publications and Data Sets} \hspace{.5em} Once manuscripts 
have the same licences as associated Research Objects, both PDF 
and machine-readable text representations of the written documents 
can be freely distributed within open-access data sets.\\\vspace{-12pt}
\hspace{7pt}\raisebox{-11pt}{\MySquare{}}\hspace{12pt}\parbox[t]{21cm}{%
{\fontfamily{fvs}\fontseries{bx}\fontsize{14}{14}\selectfont {\color[rgb]{0.1,0.4,0.5}{This makes PDF customization 
and embedded full-text search possible}}}}\vspace{12pt}
  
\dmitem \textbf{Give Authors Greater Control} \hspace{.5em} To compensate 
for missing APC revenue, publishers can reduce their workload per 
publication by delegating more responsibility to authors.  
This can be to authors' benefit and can streamline the document-prep process. \vspace{12pt} 

\dmitem \textbf{Option to Retain Outside Help} \hspace{.5em} If authors 
do want assistance with their manuscript, funds otherwise marked for 
APCs can instead be used to hire specialists who can perform 
work needed to prepare a manuscript for publications.  Instead 
of just doing copy or digital editing, these specialists can 
take on the more complex tasks implied by the Executable 
Research Object paradigm, such as implementing dataset code
and preparing raw data files.

\end{enumerate}
